# The MeansOfProduction Covenant

This document outlines the shared values, principles, and agreements that guide the MeansOfProduction community. We, the contributors, stewards, and users of this project, enter into this covenant to ensure our collective efforts are aligned, sustainable, and productive.

Our shared purpose is to build robust, open-source, and decentralized tools that empower individuals and communities. We believe that by establishing clear, transparent, and collaborative processes, we can create a resilient and impactful commons for all.

---

### Our Shared Values

1.  **Openness and Transparency:** We commit to working in the open. All code, discussions, proposals, and decisions are public by default. We believe in the power of open collaboration and radical transparency to foster trust and accountability.
2.  **Pragmatism and Quality:** We are a community of builders. We value shipping useful, well-tested, and maintainable software. The quality of our work is a direct reflection of our commitment to the project.
3.  **Respectful Collaboration:** Every voice has value. We treat all community members with respect, regardless of their background, experience level, or role. We actively seek to create an inclusive and welcoming environment for everyone.
4.  **Sustainability:** We build for the long term. Our processes and decisions prioritize the health and longevity of the project, ensuring it can thrive and adapt beyond the contributions of any single individual or group.
5.  **Merit-based Contributions:** Contributions are valued based on their merit and impact, not on a contributor's status or position. We encourage and recognize meaningful work that advances our shared goals.
6.  **Principled as Libraries:** We believe in the foundational principles of public libraries. All resources, including software, documentation, and knowledge, should be freely available to the public and designed to benefit all members of the commons, not just a select few.

---

### Our Agreements

1.  **Code & Contributions:** All technical contributions will be made through public pull requests that adhere to our established coding standards, including type checking and testing. Code is considered a public good of the project.
2.  **Decision-Making:** Decisions will be made through a transparent and consultative process. For significant changes, proposals should be clearly articulated and open for a period of discussion to allow for rough consensus to form.
3.  **Conflict Resolution:** Should a conflict arise, we will first attempt to resolve it through direct, respectful communication. If an agreement cannot be reached, the community may designate a small, neutral group to mediate and propose a solution.
4.  **Resource Allocation:** Any shared resources, such as funding or project management tools, will be managed with full transparency. Decisions on their use will align with our shared purpose and be subject to community oversight.
5.  **Community Stewardship:** The health of the community is paramount. We agree to actively participate, mentor new members, and hold each other accountable to the principles of this covenant.

---

### Conclusion

This covenant is a living document, a foundation for our collective journey. It reflects our current understanding of what is needed to build and sustain a healthy, productive community. By upholding these values and agreements, we strengthen our ability to create meaningful tools and contribute to a more open future.
